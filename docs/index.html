Features

Everything YouTube should have had

Small, focused tools that live right in the player and the toolbar. Turn on only what you want.

Repeat

Replay a video 1–5 times or forever, right from the player bar. Right-click toggles unlimited.

HD thumbnails

Open the best-quality thumbnail of any video from the player, the popup or the right-click menu.

Speed control

Presets from 0.25× to 4×, scroll to fine-tune, and a default speed for every video.

Quality & codecs

Preferred quality for videos, playlists and full screen. Force 30 FPS or H.264 on older hardware.

Volume boost

Up to 6× louder with a built-in limiter, plus scroll-to-change volume over the player.

Screenshots

Capture frames at the video's full resolution as PNG or JPG. Download, copy, or both.

Cinema, PiP & expand

Dim everything around the video, pop it out in Picture-in-Picture, or fill the whole window.

Mini player

Keep watching in a corner while you scroll to the comments, with its own size and quality.

Cleaner layout

Hide Shorts, comments, chat, related videos and end cards. Set videos per row, turn off autoplay.

Video filters

Brightness, contrast, saturation, hue, grayscale, sepia and more, plus horizontal and vertical flip.

Keyboard shortcuts

Repeat, screenshot, cinema and thumbnail on Alt+Shift keys, all customizable.

Private by design

No accounts, analytics or servers. Settings stay in your browser, with export and import for backups.

Screenshots

Looks at home in light and dark

A quick popup for everyday controls and a full settings page for the rest.

Installation

Up and running in a minute

Works in Google Chrome and Brave (version 120 or newer).

Download

Get youtube-enhancer-v1.0.1.zip from the latest release and extract it.

Open extensions

Go to chrome://extensions or brave://extensions and turn on Developer mode.

Load unpacked

Click Load unpacked and pick the extracted folder, the one with manifest.json.

Pin it from the puzzle-piece menu so the popup is always one click away.
