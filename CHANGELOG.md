# Changelog

All notable changes to **Enhancer+ for YouTube** are listed here.

## v1.0.0 (2026-10-10)

First public release.

### Added
- 🔁 **Repeat** button in the player: Off, 1–5 times, or Unlimited (right-click toggles Off/Unlimited, `Alt+Shift+R`)
- 🖼️ **HD thumbnail**: open the best available thumbnail from the player, the popup, the right-click menu or `Alt+Shift+T`
- ⏩ **Speed control**: presets, scroll-to-fine-tune, default speed for every video
- 🎞️ **Playback quality** for videos and playlists, a separate full-screen quality that switches back on exit
- 🧩 **Codecs & formats**: force standard frame rates (24/25/30 FPS) and/or H.264
- 📺 **Mini player** while scrolling (size, corner and its own quality)
- 🔊 **Volume boost** up to 6× with a limiter, scroll-to-change volume
- 📸 **Screenshots** at full video resolution: download, copy, or both (PNG or JPG, `Alt+Shift+S`)
- 🎬 **Cinema mode** (`Alt+Shift+C`), **Picture-in-Picture** and an **expanded player** that fills the window
- 🧹 **Cleaner layout**: hide related videos, chat, comments, Shorts and end screens; videos per row; autoplay off
- 🎨 **Video filters**: brightness, contrast, saturation, hue, grayscale, sepia, invert, blur, flip
- 💾 **Settings backup**: export and import
- 🌗 Popup and settings page in light and dark mode
